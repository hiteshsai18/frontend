Scandinavian Cruise Packages from India: Sail Through the Land of the Midnight Sun

Traveling is one thing; however, the kind of journey that permanently alters one’s worldview belongs to the latter class. A cruise through Scandinavia is one of these trips. Experiencing a Scandinavian cruise would be like living through every sun god’s rise and set on the big screen every day, as the Scandinavian cruise packages from India offer the most beautiful views of Iceland’s volcanic landscapes and the serene charm of Nordic cities. The Magic of the North Picture yourself being carried away through idyllic fjords; cliffs that go straight between the sea and sky, and fishing hamlets that might have been part of the landscape only as painted. This is the very embodiment of Norway. A cruise through Scandinavia gives you a chance to check out ships like Bergen and Geiranger. Each has a different pace and tale to tell. Then there’s Iceland, where geysers burst haphazardly while waterfalls plunge into lush valleys. The gorgeousness is raw and almost dreamlike. If you are very lucky, then the moon might be up playing the sun’s role when you are there in that wonder of the North, where the sun doesn’t quite vanish, and the whole place is lit with a golden tone long after midnight’s clocking in. A larger-than-life experience is guaranteed when you choose a midnight sun Iceland and Norway trip. Then you'd be there just watching nature's play, with no filters, no people, just light, sky, and silence. The Upside of Taking a Cruise Let me say it clearly: Scandinavia is not going to be affordable if you are going to resort to the city-by-city approach for planning your trip. On the other hand, cruise vacations are for the most part trouble-free. You only need to unpack once, and the boat turns into your hotel on the move. Between the ports, you will enjoy the onboard à la carte dining, entertainment, and the type of leisurely travel that is very absorbing. First stop at Oslo, Norway, to have a look around and savour the local deliciousness. Then it's off to Helsinki and finally to Stockholm and Copenhagen. It all depends on the cruise. A typical cruise through Scandinavia includes a visit to Norway, Sweden, Denmark, and sometimes even Iceland. The starting point for most itineraries is Copenhagen or Helsinki. Some itineraries also extend to include the Arctic Circle and Svalbard, depending on the time of the year. Most Scandinavian cruise packages from India include the following: • Economy Class round-trip air tickets and transfers • Schengen visa processing assistance • Cabin with meals and participation in onboard activities • Tours at the main ports of call • Northern Lights or glacier hikes as optional extras You have both the option of 7-day compact itineraries and longer 12–14 day journeys covering more ports and having more experiences. Going Deeper into Cost The next crucial question: What is the cost for a cruise to Scandinavia from India? The differences in season, cruise line, and inclusions can significantly impact the cost; however, a premium experience on average will cost around ₹3.5 to ₹6 lakh per person as the base price. The summer months (May to August) are the priciest times, as that is when the Midnight Sun shines at its brightest, and a lot of people want to see it. A Scandinavian cruise is costly, but it’s still good value considering the entire experience. Instead of paying for separate flights, hotels, meals, and local tours, you get it all in a package with seamless logistics and comfort. For Indian tourists, it means that there are fewer planning hassles and more time to enjoy the landscape. Right Time to Visit If your wish is to chase sunbeams at midnight, then the period between June and August is the perfect one for you. The Arctic glow is at its most intense then, and the weather remains warm. Alternatively, for the ones who like cooler conditions and smaller crowds, May or September is the ideal time, particularly for taking pictures, as these months have soft light and golden skies which photographers love. Besides the images, there is a sensation. That feeling of quiet amazement when you comprehend how perfectly people and nature live together in this place. This is what keeps tourists returning again and again. The Bottom Line If you think of traveling as a mix of luxury and nature with cultural richness, then Scandinavian cruise packages from India can provide it all.
